About Avery Lake

Swiss-Canadian conceptual artist & ethicist exploring what remains profoundly human in the Intelligence Age. With a doctorate in bioethics and postdoctoral research into AI ethics and human enhancement, Lake creates immersive experiences examining what remains irreducibly human when everything becomes data.

THE SIGNAL is Lake's second album, following Singular Dust (2024), which explored the tension between natural rhythms and digital frontiers.

About THE SIGNAL

THE SIGNAL is the musical companion to The Digital Audit of the Human Condition - an interactive web experience exploring what remains human when everything becomes data. Each track corresponds to a protocol (00 to ∞), examining digital behaviors while revealing deeper existential truths about identity, consciousness, and our relationship with technology.

"In the midst of technological transformation, we are searching for what it means to remain human. This project is that search."
— Avery Lake
"We do not just look at technology;
we live inside its logic."
— Avery Lake

The Digital Audit × THE SIGNAL

Each protocol in The Digital Audit examines a familiar digital behavior while revealing a deeper truth. Each track on THE SIGNAL provides the sonic landscape for these explorations.

Protocol Track Digital Behavior Existential Meaning
00 The Silence Recursive Algorithm Pausing, stepping away The silence of the universe. Stillness as origin.
01 The Observer Singularity Scrolling, watching Our eyes as black holes that give meaning.
02 The Pebble Digital Noir Repetitive gestures Finding meaning when life is not extraordinary.
03 The Handle The Architecture of Choice Clicking links, choosing Freedom exists within our choices.
04 The Asset Valuation Being measured, priced We are not commodities. Infinite worth.
05 The Heart Pulse of Validation Like button, notifications The love we can give—even online.
06 The Signal Superposition Curating identity Singular identity in infinite noise.
∞ The Breath Stillness Logging off, freedom What makes us different from machines.
