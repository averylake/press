THE ALBUM

THE SIGNAL is the musical companion to The Digital Audit of the Human Condition - an interactive web experience exploring what remains human when everything becomes data. Each track corresponds to a protocol (00 to ∞), examining digital behaviors while revealing deeper existential truths.

ABOUT AVERY LAKE

Swiss-Canadian conceptual artist & ethicist exploring what remains profoundly human in the Intelligence Age. Background in bioethics, philosophy, and AI ethics. Doctorate and postdoctoral research into human enhancement and artificial intelligence.
